Overall Evaluation:

The paper introduces a novel theoretical framework for counterfactual fairness, specifically addressing the question of "which attributes and individuals should be protected." The concept of principal counterfactual fairness is fundamentally new and addresses an important gap in the literature. The approach not only contributes to theoretical understanding but also proposes practical methods for achieving this fairness with minimal individual decision changes.

Novelty / Originality:

The paper demonstrates high novelty. The idea of using principal stratification from causal inference to define fairness criteria for counterfactual outcomes is original and represents a significant advancement in the field of algorithmic fairness.

Significance / Impact / Usefulness:

The proposed method has high potential impact on both theoretical and practical fronts. It advances the understanding of counterfactual fairness by considering the causal effect of sensitive attributes on outcomes, which is crucial for applications in various domains such as education, healthcare, and employment.

Technical Soundness / Correctness:

The theoretical framework is technically sound. The derivation of necessary conditions for principal counterfactual fairness is rigorous, and the post-processing approach is well-justified. However, the clarity and accessibility of the technical details could be improved for broader understanding.

Empirical / Experimental Validation:

The experiments are a proof-of-concept for the proposed methods. While they cover synthetic and real-world datasets, the scope is limited, and more extensive empirical validation would strengthen the claims. The experimental section should include more diverse datasets and baselines to better demonstrate the robustness of the approach.

Reproducibility / Transparency:

The paper should provide more transparency regarding the data and methodology used in the experiments to ensure reproducibility. Including code, data, and detailed setup instructions would enhance the paper's transparency and reproducibility.

Related Work & Positioning:

The related work is comprehensive but could benefit from a more nuanced discussion on how this work compares to existing fairness notions, especially those that consider causal effects or subgroup-specific fairness criteria.

Ethics / Broader Impacts / Safety:

The ethical considerations and broader impacts of the work are adequately addressed. However, the paper could further explore potential risks associated with applying principal counterfactual fairness in real-world scenarios and discuss strategies to mitigate these risks.

Recommendations:

Given the novel theoretical contributions and the potential for significant impact, the paper should be accepted pending substantial revisions to improve clarity, expand experimental validation, enhance reproducibility, and provide more nuanced comparisons with related work. The authors are encouraged to focus on making their technical exposition more accessible and to broaden the empirical evaluation to increase confidence in the method's practical utility and robustness.
