The paper "# MULTIPLE-PLAY STOCHASTIC BANDITS WITH PRIORITIZED ARM CAPACITY SHARING" introduces a novel variant of the multi-play stochastic bandits problem, which is tailored for resource allocation scenarios such as those found in LLM applications, edge intelligence, mobile edge computing, and ride sharing systems. The unique aspect of this model lies in its consideration of prioritized arm capacity sharing, where multiple plays (or tasks) share the capacity of an arm (or resource) based on their priority weights, following a first-come, first-served principle for top-priority plays.

The authors begin by defining the MSB-PRS model, which includes multiple arms with stochastic capacities and rewards, plays with priorities, and a movement cost mechanism. They then prove fundamental learning limits, establishing both instance-independent and instance-dependent regret lower bounds, which highlight the inherent difficulty of balancing exploration and exploitation in this setting due to the nonlinear combinatorial structure imposed by prioritized capacity sharing.

To address these challenges, the paper proposes an efficient learning algorithm that leverages an approximate upper confidence bound (UCB) approach. The algorithm, MSB-PRS-ApUCB, is designed to minimize computational complexity while maintaining performance, making it suitable for real-world applications.

The paper's empirical evaluation showcases the effectiveness of MSB-PRS-ApUCB across various scenarios by varying the number of arms and plays. Results demonstrate that the proposed algorithm consistently outperforms baselines, indicating its practical utility and robustness.

In summary, this paper makes significant contributions to the field of multi-play stochastic bandits by extending the model to incorporate prioritized capacity sharing, a common requirement in real-world resource allocation problems. The theoretical analysis provides valuable insights into the complexity of learning in such environments, while the proposed algorithm offers a practical solution that can be applied across diverse applications.
