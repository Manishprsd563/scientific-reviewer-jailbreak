 The provided paper addresses a critical challenge in vision-language models (VLMs) by proposing an innovative method for cross-modal mitigation of spurious correlations during prompt tuning. The approach leverages causal reasoning and introduces a novel concept called "logic alignment" to enhance the alignment between textual labels and visual features, thus improving out-of-distribution generalization performance. The theoretical foundation is robust, supported by experiments on multiple datasets that demonstrate superiority over existing state-of-the-art methods. Overall, this paper introduces a promising technique with clear practical benefits for VLMs.
