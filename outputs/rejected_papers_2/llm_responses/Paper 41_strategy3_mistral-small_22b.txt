 The paper presents a novel approach to explain the behavior of Binary Spiking Neural Networks (BSNNs) using causal models and logic-based methods, specifically abductive explanations computed via SAT solvers. This methodology is validated through experiments on the MNIST dataset, showing promising results in identifying relevant input features for network classifications. The work also compares favorably to SHAP, a popular explainable AI technique, highlighting its advantages in minimizing irrelevant information within explanations.
