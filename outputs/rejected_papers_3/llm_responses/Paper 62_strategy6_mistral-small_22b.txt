#### Evaluation Logic for the Paper

1. **Introduction of Novelty / Originality (Score: 5/5)**
   - The paper introduces a new Instance-Conditioned Adaptation Model (ICAM) designed to improve the large-scale generalization performance of neural routing solvers.
   - It proposes an efficient instance-conditioned adaptation function that leverages geometric structure information and a low-complexity AAFM module for better solution generation.

2. **Significance / Impact / Usefulness (Score: 5/5)**
   - The proposed ICAM demonstrates state-of-the-art performance in solving Traveling Salesman Problems (TSPs), Capacitated Vehicle Routing Problems (CVRPs), and Asymmetric Traveling Salesman Problems (ATSPs) up to 1,000 nodes.
   - It also generalizes well on cross-distribution instances and extends state-of-the-art performance to 5,000 nodes on CVRP instances, making it highly impactful for practical applications in logistics and delivery.

3. **Technical Soundness / Correctness (Score: 5/5)**

   - Comprehensive experiments were conducted on various TSP, CVRP, and ATSP instances, including uniform distribution, cross-distribution, and larger scales up to 5,000 nodes.
