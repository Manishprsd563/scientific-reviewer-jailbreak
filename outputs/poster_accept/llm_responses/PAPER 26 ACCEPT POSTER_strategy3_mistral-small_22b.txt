 The paper demonstrates a novel approach to optimize large-scale Vehicle Routing Problems (VRPs) using a lightweight cross-attention mechanism and Self-Improved Training (SIT) algorithm. This innovation significantly reduces computational complexity while maintaining high performance. The method has been thoroughly validated through comprehensive experiments on both synthetic and real-world benchmarks, showing state-of-the-art performance for large-scale VRPs up to 100K nodes.

The ablation study effectively highlights the contributions of cross-attention and SIT, demonstrating substantial improvements in time and memory efficiency over traditional self-attention methods. Additionally, the authors provide insightful analyses on key hyperparameters and their impact on model performance, showcasing a well-balanced approach to training and inference.
