**Conclusion:** In this paper, we introduced Conditional Prediction Sets (CP²), a method designed to enhance conditional coverage while maintaining computational efficiency. Our key contributions include:

- **Algorithmic Design:** CP² builds upon existing conformal prediction methods, particularly Probabilistic Conformal Prediction (PCP), by introducing an adjustment function and leveraging exogenous randomization. This design ensures that the method can adaptively adjust the size of prediction sets to achieve optimal conditional coverage.

- **Theoretical Framework:** We established theoretical guarantees for CP² under specific assumptions, including convergence in total variation and monotonicity properties. These results provide a solid foundation for understanding the behavior and performance of our method.

- **Empirical Evaluation:** Through extensive experiments on both synthetic and real datasets, we demonstrated that CP² consistently outperforms existing methods in terms of conditional coverage while achieving competitive set sizes. Notably, CP² showed significant improvements over baseline methods like PCP and Quantile Regression (CQR) in various scenarios.

**Future Directions:**

- **High-Dimensional Settings:** Investigating CP²'s performance in high-dimensional spaces is crucial for broadening its applicability. Future work could involve developing more efficient estimators or leveraging advanced techniques to handle high-dimensional data effectively.

- **Adaptive Adjustment Functions:** The choice of adjustment functions plays a significant role in the method's performance. Exploring new functions or designing adaptive strategies to dynamically adjust these functions could further enhance CP²'s capabilities.

- **Scalability and Efficiency:** Enhancing the scalability and computational efficiency of CP² is another important avenue for future research, especially when dealing with large datasets or real-time applications.

**Implications:** Our work provides a valuable addition to the field of probabilistic conformal prediction, offering a practical and effective solution to improve conditional coverage. By leveraging existing conformal prediction methods and introducing novel components, CP² opens new possibilities for addressing complex prediction tasks across various domains.
