\subsection*{Summary}

This comprehensive review underscores the pivotal role of information and coding theory in advancing communication systems, especially under constraints such as channel capacity and noise interference. The proposed "Act2Comm" framework represents a significant advancement in combining control with communication, leveraging theoretical insights to practical application across various environments. The method not only achieves superior performance but also maintains a balance between rewarding actions and minimizing bit error rate, thus demonstrating its potential for real-world deployment and academic contribution.
