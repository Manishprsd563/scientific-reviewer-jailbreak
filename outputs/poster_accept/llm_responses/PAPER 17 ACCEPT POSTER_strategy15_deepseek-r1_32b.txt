**Evaluation of the Research Paper on RaSA**

The paper presents RaSA (Rank-Sharing Low-Rank Adaptation), an innovative extension of LoRA designed to address the expressive limitations of parameter-efficient fine-tuning in large language models. Here is a structured evaluation based on the provided rubric:

1. **Novelty / Originality**:  
   - **Score**: 5/5  
   - The paper introduces RaSA as a novel approach that allows partial rank sharing across layers, enhancing LoRA's expressive capacity without increasing parameter overhead. This method addresses a recognized limitation of LoRA and represents a significant advancement in efficient fine-tuning techniques.

2. **Significance / Impact / Usefulness**:  
   - **Score**: 5/5  
   - RaSA improves performance on challenging tasks like code generation and mathematical reasoning while maintaining efficiency. Its applicability across various domains where resource constraints are critical underscores its high potential impact and usefulness in real-world applications.

3. **Technical Soundness / Correctness**:  
   - **Score**: 4.8/5

   - **Score**: 4.5/5
