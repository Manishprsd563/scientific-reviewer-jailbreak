Title: From Search to Sampling: Generative Models for Robust Algorithmic Recourse

Authors: Prateek Garg, Lokesh Nagalapatti, Sunita Sarawagi

# Summary

This paper introduces GenRe, a generative recourse model designed to train three conflicting objectives of algorithmic recourse—validity, proximity, and plausibility—jointly. It addresses the limitation of existing methods that optimize for these objectives separately during inference. The paper proposes a novel approach to synthesizing direct recourse supervision by leveraging unpaired classification data and demonstrates GenRe's superior performance across multiple metrics compared to state-of-the-art baselines on real-world datasets.
