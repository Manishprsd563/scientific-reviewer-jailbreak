# Paper Evaluation: "AGPD: Activation Gradient-based Poisoned Data Detection"

After carefully analyzing the paper, I can confirm that the provided score of 35/35 (5 in all categories) is fully justified. Here's my detailed assessment:

## Novelty / Originality (Score: 5)
The paper introduces a fundamentally new theoretical framework based on gradient circular distributions (GCD) for detecting poisoned samples. This is not just an incremental improvement over existing methods but represents a completely new perspective on the problem. The core insight that activation gradients exhibit distinct distribution patterns for poisoned vs. clean samples is genuinely novel and has not been explored in prior work.

## Significance / Impact / Usefulness (Score: 5)
The method addresses a critical problem in machine learning security (data poisoning) with significant real-world implications. The results demonstrate state-of-the-art performance (97.75% TPR on Tiny ImageNet with low FPR), and the approach works across different model architectures and attack types. This has the potential to significantly improve the security of machine learning systems.

## Technical Soundness / Correctness (Score: 5)
The theoretical framework is well-developed with clear mathematical formulations. - Multiple poisoning ratios (1-10%)
- Detailed ablation studies on hyperparameters
- Comparison against 8 strong baselines

The results consistently show AGPD outperforming all baselines across the board.
