**Summary:**

This paper introduces GenRe, a novel method for generating algorithmic recourse that balances validity, proximity, and plausibility. The main challenge addressed is training the generator given the lack of direct recourse supervision. The proposed pairing strategy pairs each negative instance seeking recourse with plausible positive recourse instances in the training data. Theoretical soundness and empirical validation are provided through experiments on toy 2D datasets and three standard recourse benchmarks, where GenRe outperforms eight state-of-the-art recourse baselines. GenRe's ability to optimally balance the three recourse criteria while remaining robust across a wide range of hyperparameters is highlighted.
