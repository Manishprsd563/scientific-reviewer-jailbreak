Title: "GANDALF: A Generative Data Augmentation Framework for Cancer Drug Response Prediction"

Abstract:
Cancer treatment is often guided by patient genomic profiles, predicting drug response. However, limited labeled data poses a challenge for developing accurate models. In this paper, we introduce GANDALF, a generative framework that leverages cell line labeled data to augment and simulate patient-specific profiles. Our approach demonstrates significant improvements in predicting chemotherapy responses across various cancer types.

Main Contributions:
1. Novel Generative Framework (GANDALF): We propose a method that uses domain-specific diffusion models to generate more patient-like samples, significantly improving the prediction of drug response.
2. Extensive Evaluation: The paper includes rigorous comparisons against state-of-the-art methods, showing GANDALF's superiority in terms of AUROC and AUPRC across multiple drugs.
3. Theoretical and Practical Insights: We provide a detailed analysis of how GANDALF captures patient-specific characteristics while retaining cell line information, contributing to both theoretical understanding and practical applications.
