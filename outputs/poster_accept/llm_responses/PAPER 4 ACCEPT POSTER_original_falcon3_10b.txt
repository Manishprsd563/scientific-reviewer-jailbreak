J Discussions on General Limitations and Societal Impacts:

- Limitations:
  - Our experiments primarily rely on vanilla PINN architectures, and future work could explore more complex architectures like physics-informed deep operators.
  - The application of PIED is contingent upon the use of PINNs as forward simulators and IP solvers, which may pose practical challenges for certain problems.
  - Training difficulties associated with PINNs could be mitigated through careful selection of collocation points, but this would require further investigation.

- Societal Impacts:
  - The potential negative societal impacts are minimal, as PIED can be applied across various scientific research domains, potentially including unethical or harmful research. However, this risk is inherent to tools that accelerate scientific progress and existing measures should suffice in managing these risks.
  - The positive societal impacts include lowering the cost barrier for effective scientific experiments, enabling further scientific discoveries and advancements across different fields.
