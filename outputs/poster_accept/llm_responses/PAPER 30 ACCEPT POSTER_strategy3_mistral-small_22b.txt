 The paper presents a novel statistical framework for ranking LLM-based chatbots, addressing significant challenges in pairwise comparison analysis and extending the Elo rating system used in Chatbot Arena. The proposed factored tie model significantly enhances the ability to handle ties, reducing errors by two orders of magnitude compared to existing methods. Additionally, the extension to model covariance between competitors enables deeper insights into performance relationships and facilitates grouping into performance tiers. The authors also address optimization challenges through novel constraints, ensuring stable and interpretable parameter estimation.

Empirical evaluations demonstrate substantial improvements in modeling pairwise comparison data, validating the framework's superiority over existing methods. To support reproducibility, the authors provide an open-source Python package, leaderbot, implementing their models and analyses. The paper is well-structured and presents clear contributions, rigorous evaluations, and practical applications.
