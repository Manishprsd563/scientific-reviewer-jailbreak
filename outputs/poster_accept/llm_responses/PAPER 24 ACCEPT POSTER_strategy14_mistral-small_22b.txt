 ### Paper: Neat Weight Embedding for Effective Multi-Objective Combinatorial Optimization (MOCO)

**Abstract:**
This paper introduces a neat weight embedding method to effectively solve Multi-Objective Combinatorial Optimization (MOCO) problems. By directly learning weight-specific representations, our method captures crucial weight-instance interactions, thereby reducing the optimality gaps. Extensive results on MOTSP, MOCVRP, and MOKP showed that our method surpasses the state-of-the-art neural methods and even manifests favored cross-size generalization capability.

Besides, the application of more sophisticated sampling and learning techniques across different problem sizes could further improve generalization.
