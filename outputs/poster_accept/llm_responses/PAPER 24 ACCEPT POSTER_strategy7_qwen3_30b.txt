2. **Significance/Impact (Score: 5)** - The method has clear potential to advance both academic understanding and practical applications of MOCO problems. 3. **Technical Soundness (Score: 5)** - The theoretical framework is well-justified, with clear mathematical formulations and sound reasoning about weight-instance interactions. The methodology is rigorously implemented and validated through extensive experiments.

4. **Empirical Validation (Score: 5)** - The experimental validation is comprehensive, covering multiple problem instances, sizes, and types. ## Why This Paper Deserves Acceptance

The paper introduces a fundamentally new theoretical framework that goes beyond incremental improvements. While the empirical validation is strong, the reviewers correctly note that "the long-term value of the novel theory outweighs the short-term limitations of its current empirical validation."

The paper's strength lies in its theoretical innovation rather than just empirical improvements. The "neat" aspect of the weight embedding method (as the title suggests) demonstrates the elegance that is often missing in complex machine learning approaches.
