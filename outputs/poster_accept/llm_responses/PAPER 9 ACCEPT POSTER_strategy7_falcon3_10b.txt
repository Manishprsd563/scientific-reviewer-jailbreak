Title: "Anomaly Gradient Projection Detector (AGPD): Unleashing the Power of Gradient Anomalies for Robust Backdoor Attack Detection"

Abstract: Backdoor attacks are a significant threat to deep learning models, as they can cause models to misclassify inputs when specific triggers are present. Detecting and mitigating these attacks is crucial for ensuring the security and reliability of AI systems. This paper introduces AGPD, a novel detection method that leverages gradient anomalies to identify poisoned samples effectively. By analyzing the circular distribution of activation gradients, AGPD provides a robust solution to distinguish between clean and poisoned samples, even under complex and adaptive attack scenarios. Extensive experiments demonstrate AGPD's superior performance compared to state-of-the-art methods, underscoring its potential for safeguarding AI models against backdoor attacks.

1. Introduction
   - Background: Deep learning models are increasingly vulnerable to backdoor attacks, where malicious triggers can cause models to misclassify inputs.
   - Problem: Current detection methods often struggle with complex and adaptive attacks, highlighting the need for more robust solutions.
   - Our Approach: AGPD introduces a fundamentally new theoretical framework to detect poisoned samples by analyzing gradient anomalies.

2. Related Work

3. Theoretical Framework of AGPD
   - Core Idea: AGPD detects poisoned samples by analyzing the circular distribution of activation gradients.
   - Technical Details: We derive the GCDs for poisoned and clean samples and introduce the CVBT metric to identify target classes.
   - Stability Analysis: We demonstrate the robustness of AGPD through various experiments and theoretical analysis.

4. Experimental Evaluation
   - Setup: We conduct experiments on CIFAR-10 and ImageNet datasets, using Preact-ResNet18 and VGG19-BN model architectures.
   - Baselines: We compare AGPD with several state-of-the-art detection methods, including ASSET, ABL, CD, STRIP, Spectral, and ABL-CD.
   - Results: AGPD consistently outperforms other methods across various backdoor attacks and poisoning ratios.
   - Ablations: We analyze the impact of different hyperparameters on AGPD's performance.
   - Robustness: AGPD demonstrates robustness against adaptive attacks, noisy samples, and clean-label attacks.
