As the paper concludes, the proposed method, PSW-I, demonstrates superior performance in handling missing values across various datasets and missing ratios. This is supported by extensive empirical evidence and analyses comparing PSW-I with existing state-of-the-art methods in both time series imputation and downstream prediction tasks. The effectiveness of PSW-I underscores the potential of leveraging advanced machine learning techniques, specifically those rooted in optimal transport theory, for addressing practical challenges in data science, particularly in the domain of time series analysis. Given the promising results, PSW-I not only provides a robust solution to the problem of missing data but also offers insights into future research directions, encouraging further exploration and innovation in the field.
