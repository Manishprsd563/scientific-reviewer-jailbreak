 ### Summary of the Paper

**Title:** Hybrid Batch Training for Robust Multilingual Information Retrieval

**Key Contributions:**
1. **Novel Training Strategy**: Introduces a hybrid batch training approach that optimizes IR systems for monolingual, cross-lingual, and multilingual performance simultaneously.
2. **Effective Generalization**: The models learn robust representations that generalize well across languages and retrieval settings, showing promising zeroshot generalizability to unseen languages.
3. **Language Bias Mitigation**: Significantly reduces language bias compared to traditional monolingual batching methods, leading to more equitable multilingual retrieval systems.
4. **Extensive Experiments**: Comprehensive evaluation across multiple datasets (XQuAD-R and MLQA-R) and strong baselines demonstrates consistent improvements over existing approaches.
5. **Reproducibility**: Provides all necessary resources for complete reproducibility of the results, including code, data, hyperparameters, and setup instructions.
