 The paper presented provides a comprehensive approach to optimizing information retrieval (IR) systems for multilingual performance. Here's a summary of the key points:

### Main Contributions
1. **Hybrid Batch Training Strategy**:
   - Introduces a hybrid batch training strategy that combines monolingual and cross-lingual question-answer pairs to fine-tune multilingual language models.
   - This approach aims to enhance the robustness of IR systems across various retrieval settings, including monolingual, cross-lingual, and multilingual scenarios.

2. **Performance Improvement**:
   - Through extensive experiments, the proposed method consistently matches or outperforms models trained solely on monolingual or cross-lingual data.
   - Significant improvements are observed in reducing language bias, particularly when compared to traditional monolingual batching methods.

3. **Language Bias Mitigation**:
   - The approach substantially reduces language bias by bringing relevant documents closer together in the retrieval results, regardless of the language.
   - Demonstrates competitive performance in reducing average rank distances compared to strong cross-lingual baselines.

### Experimental Results
- **Datasets**: The experiments cover multiple datasets (XQuAD-R, MLQA-R) and various languages (e.g., Greek, Vietnamese).
- **Metrics**: Evaluations include Mean Average Precision (MAP), Recall @1, and Rank Distances among relevant documents.
- **Zero-Shot Generalization**: The method shows strong zero-shot generalizability to unseen languages, highlighting its effectiveness in real-world scenarios.

### Limitations and Future Work
- While the approach focuses on improving retrieval performance, it does not address issues related to result diversity, fairness, or transparency in multilingual settings.
- The experiments are limited to specific benchmark datasets. Broader applicability across different domains and retrieval scenarios is highlighted as an exciting future direction.

### Conclusion
The work introduces a simple yet effective training strategy that optimizes IR systems for multiple languages simultaneously. By fine-tuning models on hybrid batches, the method demonstrates significant improvements in multilingual retrieval performance and language bias mitigation.
