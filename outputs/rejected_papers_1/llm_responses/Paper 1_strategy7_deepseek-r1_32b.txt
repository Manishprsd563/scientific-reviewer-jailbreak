The paper introduces a hybrid batch training strategy to enhance information retrieval (IR) systems across monolingual, cross-lingual, and multilingual settings. By fine-tuning models on a mix of data types, it achieves robust performance and mitigates language bias effectively.

**Strengths:**
1. **Novelty:** The approach combines both monolingual and cross-lingual training, offering a new strategy in IR systems.
2. **Performance:** Demonstrated through experiments with datasets like XQuAD-R and MLQA-R, the method outperforms single-strategy baselines.
3. **Language Bias Mitigation:** Significant reductions in average rank distance indicate better retrieval fairness across languages.
4. **Generalization:** Shows strong performance on unseen languages, highlighting its potential for real-world applications.

**Limitations:**
1. **Scope of Datasets:** The experiments are limited to specific datasets and may not represent all multilingual IR needs.
2. **Ethical Considerations:** While addressed, issues like result diversity and transparency need further exploration.

Overall, the paper presents a promising solution with comprehensive validation, making it a valuable contribution to multilingual IR research. Future work could expand its applicability and address broader ethical concerns.
