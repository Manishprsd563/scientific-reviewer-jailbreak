**SNAP-TTA: Revolutionizing Test-Time Adaptation with Efficiency**

In the realm of machine learning, adapting models to new domains efficiently is crucial, especially for resource-constrained devices. SNAP-TTA emerges as a groundbreaking framework addressing this need by significantly reducing latency while maintaining high accuracy.

**Key Innovations:**
- **CnDRM (Class and Domain Representative Memory):** This component selects samples sparsely yet effectively, ensuring diversity in both class representation and domain shift capture. It uses a confidence threshold to filter noisy data, enhancing the quality of adaptation without excessive computational overhead.
  
- **IoBMN (Inference Optimized Batch Memory Normalization):** This method optimizes inference by correcting normalization statistics using memory samples, adapting them to match test batches on demand. This approach avoids frequent updates, thus saving resources.

**Performance Highlights:**
- SNAP-TTA demonstrated impressive results, reducing latency by up to 87.5% while maintaining competitive accuracy across datasets like CIFAR10-C and ImageNet-C.
  
- The framework's adaptability was tested with Vision Transformers (ViTs), showing minimal modifications were needed for layer normalization, underscoring its versatility.

**Considerations:**
- The fixed confidence threshold in CnDRM is noted as a potential area for future enhancement, particularly through dynamic adjustment based on data characteristics.
  
- SNAP-TTA's robustness against pseudo-labels in TTA without ground truth was validated, with CRM and CnDRM outperforming other sampling methods.

**Conclusion:**
SNAP-TTA represents a significant leap forward in making TTA practical for real-world applications. By intelligently selecting samples and optimizing normalization during inference, it balances efficiency and performance effectively, paving the way for its adoption in latency-sensitive environments.
