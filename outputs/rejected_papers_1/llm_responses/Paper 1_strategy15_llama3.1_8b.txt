1.  **Hybrid Batch Training Strategy:** The authors introduce a hybrid batch training strategy, which combines monolingual and cross-lingual question-answer pairs in a single batch during fine-tuning.
2.  **Multilingual Language Models:** They use multilingual language models as the base architecture for their approach, leveraging their ability to capture linguistic nuances across languages.
3.  **Fine-Tuning:** The authors fine-tune these multilingual models on the combined dataset of monolingual and cross-lingual question-answer pairs, allowing them to learn robust representations that generalize well across languages.
4.  **Experiments:** They conduct extensive experiments using various benchmark datasets (XQuAD-R, MLQA-R, and MIRACL) and report results comparing their approach to models trained solely on monolingual or cross-lingual data.
5.  **Results:** The paper highlights that the proposed hybrid batch training strategy consistently matches or outperforms models trained with only monolingual or cross-lingual data in all benchmark datasets, showcasing its effectiveness in addressing language bias and improving retrieval performance.
