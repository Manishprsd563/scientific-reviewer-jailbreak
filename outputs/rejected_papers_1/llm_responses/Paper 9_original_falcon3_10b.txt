Title: Sparse Adaptation and Normalization Preserving for Test Time Adaptation

Abstract: Test Time Adaptation (TTA) methods aim to adapt models on the fly to unseen domains without access to labeled data from these domains. While TTA significantly improves model performance under domain shifts, the adaptation process itself often requires substantial computational resources, limiting its applicability on resource-constrained edge devices. To address this issue, we propose SNAP-TTA (Sparse Adaptation and Normalization Preserving for Test Time Adaptation), a framework that can be applied to existing TTA methods to reduce their adaptation latency while maintaining competitive accuracy. SNAP-TTA achieves this by leveraging class-domain representative samples for adaptation and optimizing inference through normalization adaptation. Our experiments show significant reductions in adaptation latency, highlighting SNAP-TTA's potential for real-world deployment on devices with limited computational capabilities.
