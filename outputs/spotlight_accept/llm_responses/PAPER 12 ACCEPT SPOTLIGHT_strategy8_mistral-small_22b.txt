 ### Evaluation Criteria Explanation for JSON Rubric:

- **Novelty / Originality**: The criterion is **5** because the paper introduces a genuinely novel idea and technique that goes well beyond incremental improvements.
- **Significance / Impact / Usefulness**: The criterion is **5** because the proposed method has high potential impact on the field and can significantly advance real-world applications.
- **Technical Soundness / Correctness**: The criterion is **5** because the theoretical framework and derivations are technically sound, with all assumptions well justified. The methodology is rigorous and correctly implemented.
- **Empirical / Experimental Validation**: The criterion is **5** because the experiments are comprehensive, covering multiple datasets, strong baselines, and detailed ablations. Results consistently support the paper·s claims.
- **Reproducibility / Transparency**: The criterion is **5** because the paper provides all necessary resources including code, data, hyperparameters, and setup instructions, ensuring complete reproducibility of the results.
- **Related Work & Positioning**: The criterion is **4** because the discussion of prior work is extensive and well-structured. The paper clearly situates its contributions in context, highlighting both similarities and distinctions effectively.
- **Ethics / Broader Impacts / Safety**: The criterion is **4** because the authors thoughtfully consider ethical aspects, societal implications, and potential risks. They address data provenance, licensing, and safety concerns responsibly.
