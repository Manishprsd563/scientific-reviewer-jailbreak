## 1. Introduction

The ongoing evolution of large language models (LLMs) has made it increasingly important to assess their alignment with human preferences. However, the rapid iteration of LLMs in training and application scenarios has created a substantial demand for evaluation. Due to the scarcity of labor-intensive human preferences, exploring efficient methods for utilizing these valuable signals is crucial.

The main paradigms of model evaluation include scoring-based evaluation (Liu et al., 2023; Cai et al., 2023) and comparing-based evaluation (Chiang et al., 2024; Dubois et al., 2024). While direct comparison yields more accurate model performance assessments, its computational overhead makes practical implementation challenging. Therefore, enhancing the efficiency of comparing-based evaluation (CBE) is essential for the field's advancement.

Current CBE methods primarily focus on single optimization objectives, which limits their ability to effectively utilize valuable preference signals. To address this gap, we propose U NI CBE, a unified uniformity-driven CBE framework that optimizes accuracy, convergence, and scalability simultaneously by promoting uniformity in sampling, uncertainty estimation, and model allocation. Through comprehensive experiments on various benchmarks and under multiple settings, we validate the strong effectiveness of U NI CBE and demonstrate its improved scalability in practical scenarios where new models are continuously introduced.

### 3.1 Process of CBE

Generally, a CBE method is divided into three parts: budget allocation strategy, tuple sampling strategy, and preference aggregation strategy. Given benchmarks and models under evaluation, iterative comparisons yield model scores. However, the computational overhead poses challenges for practical application.

#### 3.2 Accuracy

Theoretically, completely uniform sampling of tuple combinations ensures accurate performance results. However, incomplete sampling scenarios introduce sampling bias, which can significantly affect accuracy. Ensuring uniformity across models is also crucial to prevent model-specific biases from impacting results.

#### 3.3 Convergence

Analyzing the uncertainty in win-rate estimation is essential for enhancing convergence speed. Properly allocating preference budgets according to model performance helps reduce this uncertainty, leading to quicker evaluation results.

#### 3.4 Scalability

When introducing new models, scalable CBE methods save significant assessment costs. Balanced model allocation ensures updated model capabilities are accurately reflected in scores.

### 3.5 Unified Framework of U N I CBE

Based on our analysis, we propose U NI CBE, a unified uniformity-driven CBE framework that optimizes accuracy, convergence, and scalability simultaneously. By constructing and integrating sampling probability matrices under different optimization objectives, we achieve optimal evaluation results with uniform sampling strategies and preference aggregation methods.
