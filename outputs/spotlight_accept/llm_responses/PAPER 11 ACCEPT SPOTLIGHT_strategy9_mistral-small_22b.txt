In the context of addressing this issue, knowledge editing has often been considered a go-to remedy by efficiently patching up the erroneous answers without altering too much of the rest in an LLM. However, as the world and its related data advance with time, new errors may appear. Hence, being able to adapt the editing techniques dynamically becomes increasingly significant.

#### 1.1 Multi-Hop Knowledge Editing Pose Practical Significance:
However, due to the deeply entangled nature of different knowledge fragments in reality, efficiently propagating the editing effect to non-directly related questions is often a daunting task. This is even more pronounced when it comes to multi-hop knowledge editing tasks such as addressed herein. Prior works have shown that non-trivial challenges come with attempting to propagate an editing effect across multiple hops in a complex question (Zhong et al., 2023; Cohen et al., 2023; Wei et al., 2024).

#### 1.2 MQ U AKE-REMASTERED:
Given that the LLM editing landscape has been dominated by MQ U AKE for some time, we present MQ U AKE-REMASTERED to offer a reliable post-fix for the dataset as well as an efficient approach (GWalk) for such complex tasks. In the process of fixing MQ U AKE, we identified significant errors that were previously unnoticed and addressed them. We then demonstrated via comprehensive benchmarking on a variety of models and editing intensities how our GWalk outperforms other proposed methods significantly without relying on the original flawed dataset.

#### 2 Published Results:
Results from our experiments show that GWalk is performant, practical, and capable of delivering state-of-the-art results. We believe it can serve as a foundational baseline for future multi-hop knowledge editing studies.
