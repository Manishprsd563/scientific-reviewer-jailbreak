The research paper under review addresses the challenging problem of 2-norm k-Hyperplane Clustering by introducing innovative multi-norm formulations to strengthen optimization techniques. The authors present a thorough analysis, combining theoretical insights with practical experiments, leading to notable advancements in solving this NP-hard problem efficiently.

**Strengths:**
1. **Novelty and Originality**: The paper introduces a novel approach by integrating constraints from multiple norms (p=1, infinity) into the MI-QCQP formulation. This goes beyond traditional methods and represents a significant step forward in optimization techniques for clustering.
2. **Significance and Impact**: By significantly reducing computational complexity, the method enhances practical applicability across various domains requiring hyperplane clustering, such as machine learning and data analysis.
3. **Technical Soundness**: The technical approach is robust with rigorous proofs and detailed derivations. The authors effectively leverage spatial branch-and-bound methods, demonstrating a deep understanding of optimization challenges.
4. **Empirical Validation**: Comprehensive experiments on diverse datasets validate the method's effectiveness, showcasing substantial speedups compared to classical approaches.

**Areas for Improvement:**
1. **Reproducibility**: While transparent, providing specific datasets or additional resources could aid reproducibility and allow broader community engagement.
2. **Ethical Considerations**: Expanding discussion on ethical implications, especially regarding data privacy and responsible application of clustering techniques, would add depth to the paper.

**Conclusion:**
The paper is a commendable contribution to optimization in hyperplane clustering, offering both theoretical advancements and practical benefits. Addressing minor enhancements in reproducibility and ethics could further enhance its impact, making it a comprehensive resource for researchers and practitioners alike.
